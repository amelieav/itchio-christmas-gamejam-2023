## How did you implement the Theme and Special Object?
The Christmas theme is woven into the story and our assets show a Christmas village with presents, made of festive gingerbread and candycane.
The special object, the anvil, is the main character who aims to collect coal for his blacksmith by being naughty and destroying everything.
